# Media terms

The generated art, cutouts, soundtrack, and sound effects in this repository
are all rights reserved and view-only. They may be viewed and played with the
game. They may not be reused, adapted, redistributed, or used for
machine-learning training.

These terms cover:

- the shared edition media tree under `shared/`
- the Web Edition public assets under `apps/web/public/assets`
- the built site produced from those assets

A fork must generate its own art and music. Contributed media is accepted only
under these same terms.
