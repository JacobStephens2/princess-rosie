# Keep the seventh Birthday Star at the Castle

The journey has six flyable Places but its opening, title, and celebration promise seven Birthday Stars. Six Stars scatter across Fairytale Sicily and open six Rainbow Paths when Princess Rosie gathers them; Dad keeps the seventh, the Castle Star, safe at the Birthday Castle, where it is already shining before the six recovered Stars join it in a visible seven-Star arc or constellation over the celebration. This keeps the seven-Star promise without inventing a seventh passage or claiming that Princess Rosie gathered a Star the player never encountered.

## Consequences

- The opening distinguishes the six scattered Stars from the Castle Star.
- Every flyable Place still contains one Family Guest, Birthday Star, and Rainbow Path; Dad remains at the Castle.
- The Birthday Castle approach shows six returning Rainbow Paths, and the celebration shows all seven Birthday Stars together.
- The final gathered Star keeps the extended final cadence even though it is the sixth recovered Star.
