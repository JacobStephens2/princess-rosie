# Keep one game mode and answer low flight with a floor and a Near Miss

Manual play of Pellegrino Peak (#75) showed the Playful Bump was the least enjoyable thing in the place: nothing about it was fun to meet and nothing was fun to avoid, because a bump was not an encounter with anything but a height test — contact fired below 0.70 of a corridor reaching only 0.86, so every place's low rung and the lower half of its high rung sat inside the bump zone, and flying to a low delight and taking a bump were the same act. The journey will therefore have exactly one game mode: Gentle Help and the Cloud Rest that delivered it leave the MVP, the Playful Bump is confined to a Bump Floor that can never overlap the Altitude Ladder, and low flight is answered by an earned Near Miss rather than by assistance.

This supersedes ADR-0004's three-bump Cloud Rest clause and ADR-0009's consequence that "Cloud Rest remains a brief consequence after nearby Playful Bumps". It upholds and enforces ADR-0012: "no height is correct and none is inferior" was contradicted by a bump zone covering 78% of reachable height, and becomes a launch-time invariant rather than a sentence.

## Consequences

- There is no adaptive assistance. Gentle Help's easing was never shown to the child and its wider corridor and smaller Stella existed only to catch the bump loop; a child who finds Flight Control hard now gets the same journey as everyone else, and the journey is built to be worth flying at any height.
- Cloud Rest leaves with it. Without Gentle Help to deliver, it was a 1.2-second pause that returned Stella to the height she left and changed nothing. The three-bump rule goes with it: a Playful Bump is a lone wobble that never accumulates. `#51` stories 25 through 28 leave the MVP.
- The Bump Floor is declared as a margin above the lowest reachable height, and the journey refuses to launch if it overlaps the lowest rung of any place's Altitude Ladder — the same refusal ADR-0012 already applies to a rung naming no reachable height. A hazard that eats the ladder becomes impossible rather than merely discouraged.
- The Near Miss is promoted from an unearned route milestone to the journey's one reward for flying well. It sounds on the upward crossing out of the band, so it is never announced before the miss is a fact, and it rests between announcements for the reason ADR-0012 gives for the Abbey's bells. It answers in every place, including Rosalia's Rose Garden, whose approved `near-miss-rose-garden.wav` could not previously play because one flag gated both the bump and the near miss.
- Three `cloud-rest-*` masters retire. `playful-bump-rose-garden.wav` remains deliberately unplayed, as the Rose Garden's floor does not bump.
- Whether the Playful Bump survives at all is left open. It is kept on probation because ADR-0005 set out to replace the Original Edition's repeated obstacle pattern and the bump is what remains of it; the Near Miss is what makes low flight meaningful either way, and it survives the bump's removal. The Bump Floor's width and the Near Miss band go to feel under ADR-0007, judged first at Golden Bell Abbey, whose four rungs span the whole corridor.
