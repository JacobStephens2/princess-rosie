# Give each place a distinct gentle play vignette

> The Path Choice, route-specific celebration, and Journey History portions of this decision are superseded by [ADR-0009](./0009-prioritize-flight-control-over-mvp-path-choices.md). The distinct place-vignette decision stays active.

Replace the Original Edition's repeated obstacle pattern with seven place-specific one-button interactions: awakening roses in Rosalia's Rose Garden, a Path Choice through Zélie's Lacewood, playing golden bell notes at Golden Bell Abbey, a Path Choice through the Cloister of Clouds, riding flower-petal updrafts over Pellegrino Peak, a Path Choice across the Sapphire Sea, and accompanying the returning Rainbow Paths into the Birthday Castle. Playful Bumps remain soft and non-threatening, Birthday Stars reward direct contact but joyfully fly to Stella when she passes nearby, and no interaction can be failed or require backtracking.

The Birthday Castle celebration reflects the three Path Choices and responds to the same hold-or-release control, with separate actions to dance again or fly again. The game keeps only private local Journey History and presentation preferences, has no accounts, analytics, cloud state, scoring, streaks, or online dependency, and places sound, window mode, replay, and history reset in a secondary Grown-up Corner rather than a conventional settings screen.
